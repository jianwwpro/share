Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026
In one sentence: KagePlayer does not collect, upload or analyse any of your personal data. Your media, sentence splits and playback positions stay on your own device.

1. What we collect

We collect no personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK, and it sends no data to any server.
The following is stored only on your device:

  • Folder paths, file names and durations you import or mount
  • Sentence split points you create manually or automatically
  • Playback position and last-opened time for each item
  • Preferences such as speed, sentence mode and language

2. File access

The app only accesses files or folders you pick in the Files picker. For external folders, access is retained through a system bookmark. Audio analysis runs entirely on-device.

3. Microphone and speech recognition

This version never opens the microphone and never records. If a future version adds voice recording, it will ask for permission first and this page will be updated.

4. In-app purchase

The one-time purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We never receive, store or transmit your payment details.

5. Third-party services

No third-party analytics, advertising or push services are integrated. App Store statistics and diagnostics are handled by Apple under its own privacy policy.

6. Data deletion

Deleting a material or folder also deletes its file and sentence data. Deleting the app removes everything. The 7-day trial start date is kept in the system Keychain and may survive deletion, to prevent repeated trials.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes

If data handling changes in future (for example cloud sync), this page will be updated and the app will notify you.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: w@rcplay.cn.
