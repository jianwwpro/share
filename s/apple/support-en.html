What this app does

KagePlayer is a local media player for language learners who practise shadowing. It detects the pauses between sentences, splits a long recording into individual lines, and pauses automatically at the end of each one so you can repeat it hands-free.

The app ships with no textbooks or course content. Every file is imported by you.

FAQ

1. How do I import material?
Tap the “+” button at the top right of the home screen and choose “Import folder” or “Mount folder”. You can also drop files into the app's folder directly from the Files app.

2. Which formats are supported?
Video: MP4, MOV, M4V.
Audio: M4A, M4B, MP3, AAC, WAV, AIFF, CAF, FLAC.
WMA, APE and OGG are not supported by iOS. Convert them to MP3 or M4A on a computer first.

3. The automatic split is not accurate. What can I do?
Open the editor with the scissors button at the bottom right of the player, then adjust “sensitivity” and “minimum pause”, or import an SRT / VTT subtitle file and use its timing as sentence boundaries. You can also add or delete split points by hand.

4. How do the trial and purchase work?
You get a 7-day full-feature trial. After that, a single one-time purchase unlocks everything. It is not a subscription and nothing renews automatically.

5. I changed devices. How do I restore my purchase?
Open the paywall and tap “Restore purchase” while signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

6. Does playback continue when the screen is locked?
Yes. The app supports background playback, and you can move to the previous or next sentence from the lock screen.

7. Where is my data stored?
Everything stays on your device. The app makes no network requests, uploads nothing and collects no data. Deleting the app deletes everything.
